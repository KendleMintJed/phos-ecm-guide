## Discord Server Questions
### How do I get into rushing?
- By choosing a role (1, 2, 3 or 4) and reading what they do in this guide, you can then apply for the new challenger role in the discord server, which is proof that you understand what your role does. (https://discord.gg/khkwFecZ8D)

- Once you’ve learnt about the role you chose, go into the guides channel so you can get a reaction role for it.

### Where do I find people to rush?
- Once promoted to new challenger you're eligible to join lobbies in the lobby channel, or host one yourself.

### How hard is it to rush?
- Quite easy once you understand what your role does! The guide might look long, but remember you only need to read about your role, everyone else knows what to do on their role, so the rotations will flow smoothly

### What are SSD/NCDR?
- Those are what we call rotations. SSD is the cash oriented one, NCDR is the xp oriented one, you can check them out yourself in the rotations channel!

### What are rotations? 
- They're a series of xp/cash efficient heists to make the best use of repeat bonus in order to reach lvl 100 and or 62.5/125m cash in the shortest time possible.

### What is ecm rushing?
- It is the act of placing down ecm devices one after another, as it prevents NPCs and cameras from raising the alarm. Allowing to complete the objectives of the map as fast as possible without having to worry about being detected.

### What are pre-skills and skill breaks?
- These are moments where instead of going directly to the next heist, you instead get a bunch of skills, to make the next heists possible/easier. 
- Pre skills refer to getting a few skills, shown in your role section, before you do the first set of heists (before Diamond store for SSD, before Depot for NCDR)
- Skill breaks refer to getting a bunch of other skills (again, shown in your role section) between heist sets (after Diamond store in SSD, after Depot for NCDR)

### Do I need to be infamy 25 to rush?
- Whilst obviously recommended, since you'll have max infamy bonuses, you can start at whatever infamy. 
- If you're missing XP bonuses, ask your teammates to do a few jewellery stores before the rotation begins, until you're level 27. So that you can get the pre-skills

### Do I need any gamepasses to rush?
- No. Weapon gamepasses aren't needed at all, and the rotations can be done with an XP or cash booster to replace the gamepass, eliminating any need for them. Although variations of SSD/NCDR for gamepass-owners exist, if you have them.
