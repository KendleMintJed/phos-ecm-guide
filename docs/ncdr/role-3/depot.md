# Depot

Depot is featured on all 3 versions of NCDR

1. Kill the guard in the flat are before the ramp and take his key

![](media/depot/1.png)

2. Start placing a sentry while moving up the ramp to kill all the stationary guards.\
Kill the key guard at the top area and take his key. He will spawn on the right but move towards the left during the preplanning screen

![](media/depot/2.png)

3. Open the two rightmost garages and loot 2 bags.\
Secure bags by throwing them over this fence

![](media/depot/3.png)

4. If there have not yet been 3 quickchats called, loot a 3rd bag, quickchat “Yes!” and secure it in the same way, otherwise escape
