# Introduction
Rotations are the fastest ways of getting infamies in Notoriety, and this guide will show you how to do them.

You will first be shown the SSD rotation, which will let you reach the cash goal required to infamy along with level 100 in roughly 20-25 minutes, along with the NCDR rotation which lets you get to level 100 as fast as possible without focusing on cash. Takes roughly 10 minutes.

IMPORTANT: The guide's goal is teaching you the loadout, skills, and what each player is supposed to do on each heist, however, complementing the guide with video guides, VOD's, and other resource materials is greatly recommended (which you can find in the 'SSD/NCDR - Resources' tab)



For instructions on how to navigate the guide, check the first question in the FAQ tab

The first thing you should do is join the Moonstone game's group, as that gives a permanent +15% xp and cash.\
You can join here: <https://www.roblox.com/communities/8918098/Moonstone-Games>

You should also turn off safe escape if you're planning to host, as that removes the 3s wait before you can actually escape.

KC -> keycard\
MOLLY -> molotov\

3g/6g -> 3 garages/6 garages (areas in depot)\
WH -> warehouse (the area with crates in shadow raid)\

DS -> diamond store\
AG -> art gallery\
SWEEPOT -> depot sweep (our variation of the sweep)\
SR -> shadow raid\
NC -> nightclub\
RH -> rush hour
