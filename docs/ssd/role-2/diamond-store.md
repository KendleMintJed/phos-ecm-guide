# Diamond Store (DS)

[Video tutorial](https://youtu.be/Twpe6oLZU8I)

Diamond Store is featured on the “Booster or gamepass” and “Booster and gamepass” versions of SSD.

1. Enter the store and break your designated glass cases using a molly or a grenade launcher (see the image).
2. Bag the two jewels, the furthest first, then the closest.
3. Exit the store, throw your bags to the escape zone and go into it (you can jump and throw your bags while running from the spot shown in the image).
