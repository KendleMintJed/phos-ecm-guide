# Sweepot

![](media/sweepot/summary.png)

1. Kill the spawn keyguard, grab their key and loot the spawn cola if it is there. Grab a crowbar after r3 gets one.

2. Run past 3-garage to the flat area, kill the second keyguard and grab their key.

3. Run up the ramp to 6-garage, kill the third keyguard, grab their key and put down a sentry to kill the thugs>

4. Move and throw all the bags over the blue tarp wall to the escape zone, and check the final crate spawn.

5. Help everyone else move bags and escape.
